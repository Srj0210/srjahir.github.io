About SRJahir Tech

SRJahir Tech is an independent technology platform founded and maintained by Suraj Ahir. The platform focuses on building practical digital tools, writing in-depth technical tutorials, and making technology accessible to students, learners, and independent builders.

The project started in 2024 as a personal initiative to bridge the gap between complex technology topics and everyday understanding. Instead of following trends, SRJahir Tech prioritizes long-term usefulness — tools that actually work and content that actually teaches.

Our Platforms

CloudAI is an interactive AI assistant designed for learning and exploration. It helps users understand topics through simple, conversational explanations — particularly useful for students and beginners who want clarity without jargon.

SRJ Tools is a collection of free browser-based PDF utilities. Users can merge, split, compress, convert, and secure PDF files without installing any software. It works on both mobile and desktop with a focus on privacy and simplicity.

Stocks.SRJahir.in is a stock market information platform focused on Indian markets. It provides market overviews, index data, IPO information, and stock movement insights in a clean, readable format designed for educational use.

The Blog

The SRJahir Tech Blog publishes practical tutorials and guides on cloud engineering, DevOps, Python, Docker, Linux, web development, AI, and market literacy. The content is written step-by-step with working examples, aimed at helping readers build real skills rather than just theoretical knowledge.

Current tutorial series include a 12-part AWS + Linux guide, a 12-part Docker deep-dive, a 12-part DevOps roadmap, a 12-part Python course, and standalone articles on AI, programming languages, career planning, and stock market fundamentals.

About the Founder

Suraj Ahir is a technology practitioner with hands-on experience in cloud engineering, web development, and AI integration. SRJahir Tech is a reflection of his belief that technology should be practical, honest, and understandable — not hidden behind paywalls or complicated jargon.

Every platform, tool, and article on SRJahir Tech is built and maintained by Suraj directly, with a focus on quality over quantity and clarity over complexity.

Our Values

Clarity over complexity — Every tool and article is designed to be understood by real people, not just experts.

Learning over shortcuts — We focus on building genuine understanding, not surface-level tricks.

Privacy and simplicity — Our tools respect user privacy and work without unnecessary friction.

Honest effort over hype — We grow through continuous learning and experimentation, not aggressive marketing.
