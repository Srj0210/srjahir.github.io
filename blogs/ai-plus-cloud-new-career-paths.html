AI + Cloud = New Career Paths

SRJahir Tech · 2025

AI does not work without infrastructure. Cloud provides scale, reliability, and reach.

Why This Combination Matters

AI models need compute, storage, monitoring, security, and cost control.

Emerging Roles

  • AI Infrastructure Engineer
  • Cloud Automation Specialist
  • Data Platform Engineer
  • AI Operations (AIOps)

What to Learn

Focus on fundamentals, deployment, monitoring, and responsible usage.

Careers are shifting from tools to systems ownership.
