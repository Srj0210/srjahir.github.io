Why Certifications Alone Don’t Make Cloud Engineers

SRJahir Tech · 2025

Certifications prove effort, not capability. They test knowledge, not responsibility.

The Illusion of Readiness

Many certified candidates struggle with real systems because production environments behave differently than exams.

Cloud Is About Systems Thinking

Understanding networking, failure, scaling, and cost matters more than remembering service names.

What Actually Matters

  • Building small real projects
  • Understanding failures and recovery
  • Cost awareness
  • Security responsibility

Certifications help when combined with practice. Alone, they are incomplete.
