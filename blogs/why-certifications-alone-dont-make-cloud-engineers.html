Why Certifications Alone Don’t Make Cloud Engineers

Certifications test memory. Cloud engineering tests responsibility.

Many people collect certificates but struggle when real systems fail.

1. Cloud Is About Decision-Making

Real engineers decide: architecture, cost, security, scaling.

2. Production Is Not Multiple Choice

Exams have correct answers. Production systems have consequences.

3. Employers Look for Thinking

Hiring teams ask: “What will you do when this fails?”

What Actually Makes a Cloud Engineer

  • Linux & networking fundamentals
  • Hands-on projects
  • Understanding failures
  • Cost awareness

Certifications help open doors. Skills keep you inside.
