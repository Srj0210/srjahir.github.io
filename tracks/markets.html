Markets & Investing

8 essays · ~15 hours

Most beginners lose money not because markets are rigged but because they never learned how markets actually work — cycles, psychology, and the connection between the US market and everything else. This track is that missing education.

These are essays, not tips. No stock recommendations, no signals, no 'guaranteed returns.' Just the frameworks that separate investors who survive decades from traders who blow up in months. Educational content only — not financial advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is this financial advice?

No. Everything here is educational — how markets function, why cycles repeat, what psychology does to prices. Investment decisions are yours, ideally made with a registered advisor.

Why study global markets if I invest in India?

Because Indian markets don't move in isolation. US rates, dollar strength and global sentiment flow into Nifty every single day. The foundation essay explains the wiring.

Where should a complete beginner start?

Read 'How Global Markets Work' first — it's the foundation the other seven essays build on. Then follow the order listed above.
